# Damaged, broken, wrong or incomplete items

## Damaged item
Report damage within 7 days of delivery with a description of the problem. Refund in full.

## Wrong item
If the customer received a different product, size or colour than ordered, report within 7 days. Refund in full.

## Missing parts
If an accessory or part is missing from the box, report within 7 days. Send the missing part or refund it.

## Photo evidence
A photo of the damage helps. A claim without a photo can still be approved if the description is clear.
