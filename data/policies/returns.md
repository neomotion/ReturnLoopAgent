# Returns and change of mind

## Change of mind
Unused items can be returned within 10 days of delivery for any reason. After 10 days, reject.

## Item condition
Returned items must be unused, with original tags and packaging. Worn or washed items are rejected.

## Non-returnable items
Innerwear, perishables, gift cards and personalised items cannot be returned for change of mind.

## Refund method and timing
Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days.
