# Delivery problems: parcel not received

## Delivered with proof
If the carrier says the parcel was delivered with a signature or photo proof and the customer says it never arrived, reject. The parcel was delivered.

## Lost by carrier
If the carrier marks the parcel as lost, refund in full.

## Still in transit
If the parcel is still in transit, reject for now and ask the customer to wait for delivery.

## Wrong address
If the parcel was delivered to an address different from the order address, send the case to human review.
