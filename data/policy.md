# Return and refund policy

## Change of mind
Unused items can be returned within 10 days of delivery. After 10 days, reject.

## Damaged or wrong item
Report within 7 days of delivery with a description of the problem. Refund in full.

## Not received
- Carrier says delivered with a signature or photo proof: reject, the parcel was delivered.
- Carrier says lost: refund in full.
- Still in transit: reject for now, ask the customer to wait for delivery.

## Risk rules
Apply these only to claims that would otherwise be approved.
- Refunds above Rs 20,000 always go to human review.
- Serial returners (4 or more past orders and return rate above 50%) go to human review.
- Accounts younger than 30 days asking for more than Rs 5,000 go to human review.
